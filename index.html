Character Through Sport

Develop Islamic values and strong character through the beautiful game

Respect & Sportsmanship

Honor your teammates, opponents, and referees. Play with integrity and fairness, following the Islamic principles of good conduct.

Brotherhood & Unity

Build strong bonds with your teammates. Support each other through victories and defeats, creating lasting friendships based on Islamic brotherhood.

Discipline & Patience

Develop self-control and perseverance through training. Learn to be patient with progress and maintain discipline in practice and competition.

Gratitude & Humility

Appreciate Allah's blessings in your abilities. Stay humble in victory and gracious in defeat, always remembering the source of all success.
